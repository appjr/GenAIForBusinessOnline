You will act as an **AI consultant** hired to recommend the best LLM for a specific business problem. Your job is to put at least **3 different LLMs head-to-head** on a real task, analyze the results critically, and present your recommendation to the class.

**Deliverable:** A 5–7 minute presentation to the class (no slides required — you may share your screen and walk through your findings live).

**Due:** Next class session.

## Step 3 — Design Your Test

**Use the exact same prompt for all LLMs.** This is critical — the comparison is only valid if the input is identical.

**Craft one primary prompt** that represents your business problem. It should be:
- Specific enough to produce meaningful output (not "help me with marketing")
- Complex enough that LLMs can differ meaningfully in quality
- Realistic enough that a business professional would actually use it

**Example prompt for Scenario A (go-to-market strategy):**
> *"We are launching an AI-powered contract review tool for small law firms (2–10 attorneys) in the United States. The tool automatically flags unusual clauses, suggests standard language, and reduces review time by 60%. Our pricing is $199/month per firm. Draft a go-to-market strategy including: target customer profile, top 3 acquisition channels, key messaging, and first 90-day milestones."*

**Optional follow-up prompt (run on all LLMs after the first response):**
> *"What are the 3 biggest risks to this strategy, and how would you mitigate each?"*

Document both your prompt(s) and all responses in full.

## Step 4 — Run and Document

For each LLM:

1. **Copy your prompt exactly** — do not rephrase or adjust per tool
2. **Record the response** — copy the full text (do not summarize yet)
3. **Take a screenshot** showing the tool, your prompt, and the beginning of the response
4. **Note the following:**
   - Response time (approximate — fast / medium / slow)
   - Response length (short / medium / long)

   - Did it refuse or add excessive caveats?

### 5B — Written Analysis (1–2 paragraphs per question)

1. **What did the best-performing LLM do that the others didn't?** Be specific — quote examples from the responses.

2. **Where did each LLM fall short?** What was missing, wrong, or unhelpful?

3. **Did any LLM surprise you** — either positively (exceeded expectations) or negatively (underperformed for its reputation)?

4. **Cost and accessibility:** Given what you know about pricing, is the quality difference worth the cost? What would you recommend to a small business with a limited budget?

5. **Your final recommendation:** If a business hired you as an AI consultant and asked which LLM to use for this type of task, what would you advise — and why?

## Step 6 — Prepare Your Presentation

**Format:** 5–7 minutes, presented live to the class. No slides required — share your screen and walk through your results.

**Structure your presentation as follows:**

| Section | Duration | Content |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **1. Setup** | 1 min | Business problem you chose, why it's relevant, which LLMs you tested |
| **2. Live demo or screenshots** | 2–3 min | Walk through the responses — show, don't just tell. Highlight the best and worst moments. |
| **3. Scorecard & analysis** | 1–2 min | Your ratings and key findings |
| **4. Recommendation** | 30 sec | Which LLM wins for this task and why — one clear, confident answer |

**Tips for a strong presentation:**
- Lead with the most interesting finding — what surprised you most
- Use direct quotes from the LLM responses to make your points concrete
- Be opinionated — "LLM X was clearly better because..." is more valuable than "they were all good"
- Anticipate the question: *"Would you use this output as-is, or would you need to edit it significantly before using it in a real business context?"*

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a paid subscription to complete this assignment?**
All major LLMs offer free tiers that are sufficient for this assignment. ChatGPT free (GPT-4o-mini), Claude free, Gemini free, and Meta AI are all available at no cost. Groq and Ollama are completely free. You do not need to pay anything.

**Q: Can I use a business problem from my current job?**
Yes, but do not include any confidential, proprietary, or personally identifiable information in your prompts. Use a realistic but hypothetical version of the problem.

**Q: Can I test more than 3 LLMs?**
Yes — testing 4 or 5 LLMs will strengthen your analysis. There is no penalty for going above the minimum.

**Q: What if one LLM refuses to answer my prompt?**
Document the refusal exactly — this is data. Note what it said, adjust your prompt minimally (without changing the business intent), and try again. If it still refuses, note that as a limitation.

**Q: Can two students choose the same scenario?**
Yes, but your prompts and LLM choices should differ. Comparing results across different students with the same scenario can be an interesting class discussion.
