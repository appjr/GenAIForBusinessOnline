You will act as an AI consultant hired to recommend the best LLM for a specific business problem. Put at least 3 different LLMs head-to-head on a real task, analyze the results critically, and present your recommendation to the class.

Deliverable: A 5–7 minute live presentation. No slides required — share your screen and walk through your findings.

Step 3

Design Your Test

Use the exact same prompt for all LLMs. The comparison is only valid if the input is identical. Your prompt should be specific, complex enough to reveal differences, and realistic for a business context.

Example prompt (Scenario A — go-to-market strategy)

"We are launching an AI-powered contract review tool for small law firms (2–10 attorneys) in the United States. The tool automatically flags unusual clauses, suggests standard language, and reduces review time by 60%. Our pricing is $199/month per firm. Draft a go-to-market strategy including: target customer profile, top 3 acquisition channels, key messaging, and first 90-day milestones."

Optional follow-up prompt (run on all LLMs after the first response)

"What are the 3 biggest risks to this strategy, and how would you mitigate each?"

Document both your prompt(s) and all responses in full.

Step 4

Run and Document

For each LLM:

  1. Copy your prompt exactly — do not rephrase or adjust per tool
  2. Record the full response — copy the complete text, do not summarize
  3. Take a screenshot showing the tool, your prompt, and the start of the response
  4. Note: response time (fast / medium / slow), response length, whether it asked clarifying questions, whether it refused or added excessive caveats

5B — Written Analysis

Answer each question in 1–2 paragraphs. Be specific — quote directly from the LLM responses.

  1. What did the best-performing LLM do that the others didn't? Be specific — quote examples.
  2. Where did each LLM fall short? What was missing, wrong, or unhelpful?
  3. Did any LLM surprise you — positively or negatively?
  4. Cost and accessibility: is the quality difference worth the cost? What would you recommend to a small business on a limited budget?
  5. Your final recommendation: if a business hired you as an AI consultant, which LLM would you advise for this task — and why?

Step 6

Prepare Your Presentation

Tips for a strong presentation

  • Lead with the most interesting finding — what surprised you most
  • Use direct quotes from the LLM responses to make your points concrete
  • Be opinionated — "LLM X was clearly better because..." is more valuable than "they were all good"
  • Anticipate the question: "Would you use this output as-is, or would you need to edit it significantly before using it in a real business context?"

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a paid subscription?
No. ChatGPT free, Claude free, Gemini free, Meta AI, Groq, and Ollama are all available at no cost. You do not need to pay anything to complete this assignment.

Q: Can I use a problem from my current job?
Yes, but do not include any confidential, proprietary, or personally identifiable information. Use a realistic but hypothetical version of the problem.

Q: Can I test more than 3 LLMs?
Yes — testing 4 or 5 LLMs will strengthen your analysis. There is no penalty for going above the minimum.

Q: What if one LLM refuses to answer?
Document the refusal exactly — this is data. Note what it said, adjust your prompt minimally without changing the business intent, and try again. If it still refuses, note that as a limitation.

Q: Can two students choose the same scenario?
Yes, but your prompts and LLM choices should differ. Comparing results across students with the same scenario can make for an interesting class discussion.
